# AgriNexus — AI Coding Instructions

## Project

AgriNexus is an interoperable AI-powered agricultural intelligence platform designed for climate-resilient farming across BRICS nations.

The competition prototype must demonstrate:

- Localized agro-advisories
- Agricultural/environmental data integration
- Gemini AI reasoning
- Multimodal crop disease diagnosis
- Regenerative agriculture recommendations
- Multilingual/voice-ready delivery
- Cross-border applicability
- Deployable architecture
